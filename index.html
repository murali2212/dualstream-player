📡 Bluetooth Setup Guide

Follow these steps to connect your headphones

1

Put both headphones in pairing mode (usually hold power button until light flashes)

2

Open Settings → Connected devices → Pair new device

3

Tap on Headphone 1 to pair it

4

Go back and tap Pair new device again for Headphone 2

5

Return to DualStream, tap Scan for Devices below

💡 Tip: Samsung phones have built-in Dual Audio — enable in Settings → Connections → Bluetooth → ⋮ → Dual Audio
